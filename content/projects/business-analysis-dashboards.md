Three end-to-end Power BI dashboards built on banking, toy retail and superstore
datasets. Each one turns raw transactional data into management-level metrics that
people can explore themselves.

## What it does

- **30+ interactive visualizations** across the three dashboards, covering revenue,
  sales, customers, products and operational performance.
- **Reusable KPIs and calculated measures** summarize business performance across
  multiple dimensions and turn raw transactions into trend analyses.
- **Self-serve exploration:** slicers, drill-throughs, cross-filtering and linked
  visuals let users go from high-level KPIs to the detail behind them without
  changing the underlying reports.

## How it is built

- **Power Query** to clean and shape the source datasets.
- **DAX** for the calculated measures and KPIs.
- **Power BI** for the report design and the interactions between visuals.
